On Io, we descended into the depths of the abyss with orders to bring it out. It... What was it? Our newfound relic told us about it, but nobody outside S&T knew for sure what it was. It was rumored to be a new technology upgrade resulting from the bio-metal research the Cthonians performed on Venus. They called it Project Furies. All we really knew for sure was that the Soviets were going to make a play for it. So we had to do the same. The race goes on.
