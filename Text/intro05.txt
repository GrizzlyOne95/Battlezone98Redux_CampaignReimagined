The discovery of the Pegasus device had furnished us with a prize to be won from the Chinese. Morale climbed higher as we saw a goal ahead of us, rather than a Chinese prison. The fluke destruction of the Chinese Recycler gave us an opening. We could strike deep at the heart of the Chinese base without fear of major reinforcements arriving, but we only had a little bit of time left. We struck hard and decisively at the first chance we had.
