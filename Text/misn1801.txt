Grizzly One. The Furies Factory tapped the molten core of the moon for power. Its destruction has initiated a chain reaction that has destabilized the moon. Furies are once again attempting to escape. You must destroy their transport and get back to your Recycler before the moon explodes. We've identified two approaches. First is to the west of our position. It is shorter and more direct, but heavily traveled by escaping Furies. The second route is to the north. It is longer, narrower, and has many branches. However, it has less Furies activity. Destroy the transport and get back here as fast as you can. Good luck.
