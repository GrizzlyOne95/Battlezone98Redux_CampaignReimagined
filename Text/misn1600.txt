We gathered every resource we could find, and engineering equipped us with new weapons. It seemed like a futile battle, but we had to destroy the Furies. Their numbers were increasing, and we could feel their ambitions grow. They were sentient. We knew that from the database. They loved to kill. We saw that. It was only a matter of time before they found Earth. There was nowhere to hide. Our only option was to stay and fight for our lives. And the lives of everyone back home.
