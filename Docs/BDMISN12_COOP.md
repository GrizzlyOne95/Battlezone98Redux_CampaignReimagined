# Black Dog 12 co-op: Meanwhile, Back at the Ranch... (bd12cr)

Shared offline / 2-4 player co-op port of the Black Dog mission `bd12`: defend the four shield and four power
generators of the Black Dog base on Ganymede (and the portal the engineers are building) against four timed Chinese
(CCA) raids, take the reinforcements the portal sends, and destroy the four Chinese goal buildings. One
`Missions/bd12cr.bzn` and one `Scripts/bd12cr.lua` serve both modes. Companion to
[`BLACKDOG12_LUA_PORT.md`](BLACKDOG12_LUA_PORT.md) (the faithful DLL port underneath, `Scripts/bdmisn12.lua`) and
[`CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md`](CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md) (the shared runtime). Pattern: [`MISNS2_COOP.md`](MISNS2_COOP.md),
[`BDMISN05_COOP.md`](BDMISN05_COOP.md). Status: **host-tested only**; nothing here has run in the game (see "Live
checklist").

## Teams and ownership

| Team | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 | Humans (host on 1). The authored Black Dog side (the start tank `player`, the wingmen, the shields, the power units, the portal, the armory, the scavengers) stays on team 1: shared, host-run. Extra starting recyclers are disabled. |
| 5 | The Chinese: the authored base (the four goals `goal_1`..`goal_4`, barracks, towers, turrets, the ambient wingmen) and every raider and counterattack tank. The body still says team 2; the runtime maps `BuildObject(..., 2, ...)` to team 5. |
| 7 | Support (nothing uses it here). |

Only the authority (host, or the offline player) runs the body; guests only present. `SetAIControl(5, true)` runs once
at chunk load. The mission has no `SetAIP`: the enemy is scripted (raids) plus the authored base, so there is no shared
enemy AIP pool; `SetScrap(1, 50)` becomes `50 + 10 per guest` (`scrapPerGuest`), `SetScrap(2, 10)` becomes team 5, and
`SetPilot(1, 10)` gives 10 pilots to every human team (each human respawns from its own pool).

### Humans as the player

The DLL's `user = GetPlayerHandle()` is assigned every frame and **never read**: no gate, order or camera aims at "the
player". The raids aim at the generators, the shields and the portal (shared base objects), so the nearest-human token
is not needed and `GetPlayerHandle` is left alone. (When a raider has no target left, the stock-bug fix below aims it
at the nearest human.)

## Scaling and concealed arrivals [owner rules, mission decisions]

Scaled spawns: all six Chinese combat ODFs the body builds: `cvfigh`, `cvtnk`, `cvhtnk`, `cvltnk`, `cvhraz`,
`cvwalk`. They become *tickets*: the body gets a negative request id, the unit is built by the runtime on the next
frames, and the body's later calls on it (`SetCloaked`, `Attack` against a generator at priority 1, `Defend2` of an
escort on its attacker) are replayed on it and on its extras; a `Defend2` that names another ticket is rewritten to
the real unit when that one is built.

Events. The runtime region wraps `BuildObject` once: **a new frame or a new entry path is a new scaling event**, and
the first unit of an event is mirrored by `min(floor(humans / 2), 2)` extras of the same kind, entry and orders. The
raids are: 120 s (attack_1, defend_1), 240 s (attack_2, defend_2), 360 s (attack_3, defend_3), 480 s (the five
walkers with their escorts: attack_4/defend_4 .. attack_8/defend_8), and one event per destroyed shield-or-power pair
(`despor_1`..`despor_4`, two tanks each): 16 scripted events plus up to four counterattacks, 36 primaries plus eight.

| Humans | Extras per event |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (offline) | 0 |
| 2 or 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 2 |

Cap: at most **24** live scaled units (`liveCap`; the runtime default is 12). The fourth raid is fifteen units, so with
four players it reaches the cap and drops extras (the only requests dropped for it, primaries always spawn).

No pop-in: a ticket is never built within 460 m of any human. The humans start at the Black Dog base; every authored
approach is farther than that except `despor_3` (424 m from the start, east of the base), so the counterattack for the
south-east pair would arrive in view. It uses the first clear entry of `fallbackEntries` (`attack_1`, `attack_6`,
`attack_4`, `attack_2`, `attack_7`, `attack_8`, the approaches farthest from the base first), else waits (saved in
`M.crcca`, so a save/load neither loses nor duplicates it). **Consequence, a design change:** while a human stands
within 460 m of one of a raid's own paths, that part of the raid arrives from another approach. All raiders are cloaked
at spawn except the walkers, as the body does (a ticket is cloaked when the real unit is built).

Not scaled: the portal reinforcements, which are friendly
(the shared team 1) and unchanged for any head count.

## Humans' result, timers, win and loss

- Win: unchanged. After the defence objective advances (795 s) and all four goals are dead: `bd12003`, then
  `SucceedMission(..., "bd12win.des")` once (the runtime holds the result until every guest acknowledged the preceding
  events).
- Loss: unchanged. The portal destroyed: `bd12004`, then `FailMission(..., "bd12lsea.des")` once for everyone. The
  out-of-lives result (every human out) fails everyone with the same debrief (`failDes = "bd12lsea.des"`), and a
  departed leader fails every guest.
- `wonFlags = { "won" }`, `endedFlags = { "won", "lost" }`: once the win is latched a late failure is ignored, and once
  either ending starts nothing more is built.
- The body uses the TRO 1.3 timing: the recycler at 780 s (portal open from 778 s), the escorts at 786 s and 792 s, the
  portal closing at 794 s and the defence objective advancing at 795 s. The later C++ source had the escorts at 546-554 s.

## The portal

The body drives the portal with `PortalOut`, `ActivatePortal` and `DeactivatePortal`, which are local effects of the
object. The CR layer applies them on every peer through the ordered presentation stream (`presentationOps.BD12Portal`,
each peer drives its own copy of the map object `portal`). The reinforcements themselves (`BuildObjectAtPortal`:
two `bvtank`, one `bvfigh`, one `bvrecyd`) are built by the host on the shared team and replicate; nobody travels
through this portal, so there is no per-human teleport to handle. They follow the path `follow` (`Goto`, priority 1).

## Cinematics are shared and unskippable in a network game [owner]

The opening (the shot `camera_start` around the portal, 22 s of briefing `bd12001` after three seconds) is driven by
`CameraPath` every frame, so the runtime's camera stream carries it to every guest; `CameraCancelled` is false in a
network game, offline it stays skippable. It ends when its path arrives.

**One deviation:** a skip key pressed by the *host* releases only the host's own camera and the runtime then stops
driving that shot (`CRCCARuntime.CameraPath` returns nothing once skipped); the body ends the opening only on arrival,
so the opening would never end for anyone (no objective is shown until it does).
The CR layer treats a host skip during the opening as the arrival. Only a skip raised **after the shot began** counts:
the shot starts by clearing the skip latch (`CameraCancelled(true)`), so a stale latch from before cannot end it.
Guests cannot skip. A save/load cannot resume a film (the camera is gone): on load a film that was running opens its
camera again and the body ends it as usual.

## Restored content [owner decision 2026-11-01]

`References/BlackDog12Source/BlackDog12Mission.cpp` was read end to end. **It contains no disabled or `#if 0`
gameplay**: no cut block exists to restore (the port notes say the same). The restorations are therefore unused shipped
assets and one timing repair, each behind a flag at the top of the runtime region of `bd12cr.lua`, applied offline
and in co-op.

Unused shipped voice line: `bd12002.wav` (4.93 s) and `Text/bd12002.txt` ship; the DLL never plays it. It plays once
through the CR layer (`AudioMessage`: replicated to co-op guests, queued behind a line still playing) and is latched
in `M.crcca.r` (kept by Save/Load). Flag `RESTORED_VOICE_LINES`.

| Line | Transcript | Trigger | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bd12002` | "Oh yeah, back home to roost. Now where're those commie bastards?" | Once, when the opening ends (after a skip too); not after the win or the loss | It is the Sergeant's answer to the briefing (`bd12001`: "Sergeant, protect those men at all costs"), spoken when he takes control of the base, and it asks for the enemy that arrives two minutes later. |

## Stock bugs and co-op fixes in the CR layer (the faithful body is untouched)

- **Dead target, idle raiders.** The raiders are ordered once against a generator, a shield or the portal. If that
  target is already gone when they are built (`AttackUnit` skips an invalid target) or dies before they arrive, the
  raider stands where it was built for the rest of the mission. A scaled unit with no command now attacks the nearest
  human, re-checked every 20 s (`Restored.Retarget`, uses `GetCurrentCommand`); ordered units are never touched.
- **Cloak on tickets.** The body's `SetCloaked(h)` right after the spawn would receive a negative request id: the
  shadow defers it and applies it when the real unit (and each of its extras) is built.
- **Host skip stalls the opening** (see "Cinematics"); a stale skip latch is cleared at the shot's start.
- **Portal effects are local** (see "The portal") and the portal is shut while the escorts arrive (see "Restored
  content").
- **Audio handles.** `IsAudioMessageDone` treats a nil or 0 handle as finished (the body already guards nil), so a
  shared line that returns no native handle cannot stall either ending.
- **Objectives after a load.** The runtime's Load resets the presentation; the CR layer rebuilds the whole list from
  the mission state (`bd12001` white, or green plus `bd12002` white once the defence objective advanced). The mission
  has no objective markers, names or cockpit timer to republish.
- **Pilots and scrap** per human team / per guest (see "Teams").
- **Out of lives** fails everyone with `bd12lsea.des`.
- The body already carries its own documented port fixes (`BLACKDOG12_LUA_PORT.md`: zero health for missing objects, a
  missing camera subject ends the shot, missing narration counts as completed).

## Weather: Ganymede

Ganymede, the icy airless Jovian moon: the existing `CREuropaWeather` director (no new module) with the generated
`CRGanymedeSitesBD12` sites (six cryo-volcanic vents: three under the Chinese goal buildings, three around the Black
Dog base, frost anchors, bounds), networked exactly as in `bd05cr`/`misn12cr` (seeded timeline from the host,
host-chosen events over the ordered stream with the ops `EuropaState`, `EuropaPlume`, `EuropaQuake`, `EuropaSurge`,
`Save`/`Load` state, `AnnounceState`). Lighting is the orbit mode (`period = 1800`, `startHour = -40`) and the sensor
modifier is bound once the director exists (`Environment.BindWeatherSensors("Ganymede", ...)`, again after a load). If
the weather module or the sites module is missing, or any weather call errors, the mission runs without weather and
logs once. With zero vents the plumes are simply skipped. Beats (host/offline only, each once; the director never
damages):

## Inferences and open questions

- Scaling is per (frame, entry path), a CR inference (the same rule as `misns2cr` and `bd05cr`); `liveCap = 24` is
  a guess for four humans on the fourth raid.
- The fallback order assumes the six listed raid approaches are all usable and that the humans stay near the base; a
  watched fallback is skipped for the next.
- The portal reinforcements are not scaled for the head count (friendly units; the owner's scaling rules cover enemies).
- Treating the host's skip of the opening as its arrival assumes a skip press is visible to `CameraCancelled()` in
  the same frame the runtime releases the camera, and that `CameraCancelled(true)` clears the latch.
- The retarget uses `GetCurrentCommand`/`AiCommand.NONE`; a unit that is merely between orders for a moment could be
  re-aimed once (the check runs at most every 20 s).
- The out-of-lives path is not exercised by the host tests (it is the runtime's own `Failure` callback).

## Warning re-arm after repairs

The shield and power low-health warnings (`bd12005`..`bd12008`, below 25 %) still latch, but the latch clears when the
unit is repaired above 50 %, so the warning can play again. Between 25 % and 50 % the latch holds; dead or invalid
handles never clear it. It runs on every peer in a CR region (`restored content`), and `healthLow` already exists in old
saves. Live health percentages on the shield objectives were not added: the mission has no objective markers or names.
