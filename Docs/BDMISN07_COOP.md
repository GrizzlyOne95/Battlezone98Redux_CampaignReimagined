# Black Dog 07 co-op: Terra Incognita (bdmisn07cr)

Shared offline / 2-4 player co-op port of the Black Dog mission `bd07` on Elysium: the Black Dog outpost has landed
near a Chinese (CCA) base, HQ asks it to keep a low profile, two Chinese waves are beaten, then the go-ahead comes to
destroy the whole Chinese base, harassed every five minutes by a fighter and light-tank raid. One
`Missions/bdmisn07cr.bzn` and one `Scripts/bdmisn07cr.lua` serve both modes. Companion to the faithful DLL port
underneath (`Scripts/bdmisn07.lua`, tested by `Tools/Test-BlackDog07.lua`) and
[`CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md`](CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md) (the shared runtime). Pattern: [`MISNS2_COOP.md`](MISNS2_COOP.md) and
[`BDMISN05_COOP.md`](BDMISN05_COOP.md). Status: **host-tested only**; nothing here has run in the game (see "Live
checklist").

## Teams and ownership

| Team | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 | Humans (host on 1). The authored Black Dog side (the start tank `player`, the recycler `recycler`, the factory `myfactory`, the HQ `my_hq`, the nav beacons, the base turret and buildings, three scavengers) stays on team 1: shared, host-run. Extra starting recyclers are disabled. |
| 5 | The Chinese: the authored base (the 11 structures the player must destroy and everything around them), the 14 wave units and every raider. The body still says team 2; the runtime maps `BuildObject(..., 2, ...)`, `SetScrap(2, ...)` and `SetAIP` to team 5, and the CR layer maps `GetScrap(2)` and `SetPilot(2, ...)` (see "Stock bugs"). |
| 7 | Support (nothing uses it here). |

Only the authority (host, or the offline player) runs the body; guests only present. `SetAIControl(5, true)` runs once
at chunk load. `SetAIP("bdmisn07.aip")` always drives team 5 (the Chinese base's own production and defence).
`SetScrap(1, 8)` becomes `8 + 10 per guest` (`scrapPerGuest`), `SetPilot(1, 10)` gives 10 pilots to every human team
(each human respawns from its own pool). Respawns rally on the recycler.

### Humans as the player

The DLL's `user = GetPlayerHandle()` is assigned every frame and read in one place: the harassment raid orders
`Attack(raider, user)`. In co-op the CR layer answers `GetPlayerHandle` with the runtime's nearest-human token
(`crcca_players`), so `M.user` is the token and:

- `Valid(M.user)` is true (the `IsValid` shadow knows the token); `Attack(raider, M.user)` goes to the **nearest living
  human** to that raider. A raider that is still a ticket remembers the order and is ordered the moment it is built
  (extras too).
- The body never measures a distance to the player, never reads a unit's team and never aims a camera at him, so no
  other mapping is needed.

## Scaling and concealed arrivals [owner rules, mission decisions]

Scaled spawns: the ODFs `cvfigh` and `cvltnk`, i.e. exactly the harassment raid (`annoy_1`: 3 fighters and 2 light
tanks, first at the go-ahead plus 1 s, then every 300 s). They become *tickets*: the body gets a negative request id, the
unit is built by the runtime on the next frames and the body's `Attack(raider, user)` is replayed on it and on its
extras. Not scaled: the 14 authored wave units and the Chinese base, which are map content, not spawns.

Events. The runtime region wraps `BuildObject` once: **a new frame or a new entry path is a new scaling event**, and the
first unit of an event is mirrored by `min(floor(humans / 2), 2)` extras of the same kind, entry and orders. One raid is
one event.

| Humans | Extras per raid |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (offline) | 0 |
| 2 or 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 2 |

Cap: at most **16** live scaled units (`liveCap`; the runtime default is 12): the raiders hunt until they die, so
several raids can be alive together. Extras are the only requests dropped for the cap, primaries always spawn.

No pop-in: a ticket is never built within 460 m of any human. `annoy_1` is a single point on the east edge of the map
(2145, 99485), about 1000 m from the Black Dog base. If a human stands near it, the next clear authored approach in
`fallbackEntries` is used (`attack_path2`, then `attack_path1`: the first points of the two wave paths, 1.2 km and 0.5 km
from the base), else the request waits (saved in `M.crcca`, so a save/load neither loses nor duplicates it).
Reinforcements stop for good when the mission is won or lost (`endedFlags = won, lost`); the body itself would keep
raiding until the result screen.

The two map waves are not scaled for the head count: they are the mission's fixed opening difficulty and a four-player
base meets them with four players' firepower. Scaling them would mean spawning authored units (a design change).

## Humans' result, win and loss

- Win: unchanged. All 11 Chinese structures (`chin_recycler`, `chin_factory`, three solar plants, three towers,
  supply, HQ, hangar) dead -> green objective -> `SucceedMission(..., "bd07win.des")` once (the runtime holds the
  result until every guest acknowledged the preceding events). The body's list is fixed; anything the Chinese rebuild is
  not on it.
- Loss: the recycler, the factory **and** the HQ all dead (one of them is enough to stay in the game) ->
  `FailMission(..., "bd07lose.des")`. The out-of-lives failure of the co-op layer uses the same debrief (`failDes`).
- `wonFlags = { "won" }`: a loss after the win is ignored. `endedFlags = { "won", "lost" }`: nothing is built after the
  result.
- A departed leader fails every guest.

## Cinematics

None. The mission has no camera: the briefing is three radio lines (`bd07001`..`bd07003`, the shared audio stream plays
them on every peer in order) and the objectives follow the last one. Hence no Presentation suite.

An absent audio handle counts as a finished line (it becomes 0, which `IsAudioMessageDone` accepts), so the briefing
cannot stall. The first objective shown sets `M.crcca.r.briefDone` (saved with the state): it arms the restored waves,
and a Load, which resets the presentation and drops the objectives, republishes the earned phase (patrol, destroy or
done) once the briefing has finished.

## Restored content [owner decision 2026-11-01]

Cut-content restorations live only in the CR layer, apply offline and in co-op, and sit behind `RESTORED_WAVE_ATTACK`
(top of the runtime region of `bdmisn07cr.lua`). Each is gated on what the shipped game carries.

**Unused voice lines: none.** The shipped game carries exactly `bd07001`..`bd07004` (`StockODFFiles/bd07001.wav`..,
`Config/durations.csv`, `Text/bd07001.txt`..`bd07004.txt`), all of which the DLL plays; the only other audio of the
mission is `intro07.wav`, the campaign briefing movie, not a mission line. The shipped debriefs `bd07win.des` and
`bd07lose.des` are used. There is no `bd07005` or later to slot in.

### The two Chinese waves set out [RESTORED_WAVE_ATTACK]

Source (`BlackDog07Mission.cpp`, `Setup` and `Execute`):

```cpp
	wavesSpawned = TRUE;
	...
	waveDelay[0] = 999999.9f;
	...
	if (waveDelay[0] < GetTime())
	{
		waveDelay[0] = 999999.9f;
		waveDelay[1] = GetTime() + 30.0f;

		// get first wave to attack
		for (i = 0; i < 3; i++)
			Goto(waveUnits1[i], "attack_path1", 1);
	}

	if (waveDelay[1] < GetTime())
	{
		waveDelay[1] = 999999.9f;

		// get second wave to attack
		for (i = 0; i < 8; i++)
			Goto(waveUnits2[i], "attack_path2", 1);

		wavesSpawned = TRUE;
	}
```

Nothing ever arms `waveDelay[0]`, so in the faithful port the two preplaced waves are never ordered out: the whole
attack logic, the two authored paths `attack_path1` (8 points) and `attack_path2` (25 points), and the sentence of the
briefing "keep a low profile / await further orders" lead nowhere, and the go-ahead (`bd07004`) only comes when the
player hunts the 11 wave units down himself. Both paths **end at the Black Dog base** and start where the wave units
stand, so the cut is plain: the waves were meant to attack.

The CR layer arms the body's own timer: **120 s after the objectives appear** (`bd07003` finished) `waveDelay[0]` is set
to `GetTime() + 120`; everything after that is the source code, untouched: wave 1 (`chin_scout1..3`) goes
`attack_path1` at priority 1, wave 2 (`chin_scout4..6`, `chin_ltnk1/2`, `chin_tank1`, `chin_bomber1/2`) goes
`attack_path2` 30 s later. Gated on:

- both paths existing in the map (else the faithful mission runs),
- the waves not already beaten (`objective1Complete`) and the mission not decided,
- the briefing finished (`r.briefDone`: the objectives have been shown),
- done once (`M.crcca.r.waveArmed`; kept by Save/Load, the body's own `waveDelay` timers are saved state).

The 120 s is an inference (nothing in the source or the audio places the wave): it gives the radio lines (about 9 s) and
a calm patrol before contact, in the spirit of "stay alert". The waves are map units and are not scaled (see above).

Cut or dormant blocks considered and **not** restored:

| Block | Source | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `objective2Complete`, `objective3Complete` ("2 and 3 are unused") | `NewState` / `Setup` | Never read or written. No content, nothing to restore. |
| `wavesSpawned = TRUE` in `Setup` | `Setup` | Kept as is: it is why objective 1 is evaluated from the start, and the restoration above does not touch it. |
| `Attack(h, user)` on a missing player | `Execute`, raid | Body safety guard kept; the CR layer adds the idle-raider fix below. |

Authored map content the script never uses (documented, not wired): the path `edge_path` (two points at the map corners,
(201, 102080) and (2259, 99436); it is the playable bounds, also in `CRElysiumSitesBD07`), the Chinese `chin_barracks1..3`,
`chin_cafe`, `sbcomm2_commtower`, turrets, scavengers, silos and scrap field (map-native enemy content, not on the
body's lists), the Black Dog `mysolar1/2`, `mytower1`, `mytank1`, `myrocket1`, `myscout1/2`, barracks, hangar, silo,
supply and scavengers, and the scrap pickups. **Map note:** `chin_scout1`, `chin_scout2` and `chin_scout3` are each
labelled **twice** (two trios of Chinese fighters: one north of the Chinese base, one 470 m from the
Black Dog base at the start of `attack_path1`); `GetHandle` resolves to one of each pair, so the body (and the
restored wave) orders one trio and tracks it for objective 1. The other trio is ordinary map-native enemy content
(the AIP's, if it wants them).

## Stock bugs and co-op fixes in the CR layer (the faithful body is untouched)

- **`GetScrap(2)` reads a guest.** The go-ahead tops the Chinese up to 40 scrap (`SetScrap(2, 40)` is mapped to team 5 by
  the runtime) after reading `GetScrap(2)`; in co-op team 2 is a human guest's team, so the test would read his scrap
  and could starve the Chinese. A shadow reads team 5.
- **Pilots.** `SetPilot(1, 10)` gives 10 pilots to every human team, not only team 1; `SetPilot(2, ...)` is mapped to 5.
- **"The player" is the nearest human** (see above), and a raider that is still a ticket keeps the order until it exists.
- **Idle raiders.** The body issues each raid's `Attack` once and only when the player exists; with no living human at
  that moment (or after the target died) the raider stands where it was built for ever and the raids pile up at
  `annoy_1`. Every 20 s a raider with no command attacks the nearest living human (`Restored.Hunt`); a raider that
  already has a command is left alone, and nothing is ordered after the win.
- **The waves never set out** (see "Restored content").
- The body already carries its own documented port fixes (`ShowObjectives` re-renders the current phase, null-handle
  guards, `GetHealth` of a missing structure counts as dead).

## Weather: Elysium

Elysium, the Earth-like world under a blue-grey overcast: the existing `CRElysiumWeather` director (the Venus engine
re-dressed: blue-grey fog and light, cool white-blue rain, no sulphur, vents or acid on the glass, gentler wind and
strikes) with the generated `CRElysiumSitesBD07` sites, networked exactly as Venus in `misns2cr` (seeded timeline from
the host, host-chosen strikes over the ordered stream with the ops `VenusState`, `VenusStrike`, `VenusErupt`,
`VenusFog`, `Save`/`Load` state, `AnnounceState`). Lighting is the runtime default (subtle). The sensor modifier and
the lightning scramble belong to the Venus engine inside the module, so nothing is bound here. If the weather module or
the sites module is missing, or any weather call errors, the mission runs without weather and logs once. The ladder
starts at level 1 (calm) and drifts to 2 (haze). Beats (host/offline only, each once; the director never damages, a
strike only knocks craft near it about):

| Moment | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| Wave 1 sent (restored, objectives + 120 s) | level 3 (rising) |
| Wave 2 sent (+30 s) | level 4 (storm) |
| The waves are beaten (`bd07004`, the go-ahead) | level 4 and a strike over `nav_chinbase`, the Chinese base |
| Every harassment raid | a strike over its first raider, once the raider is built |
| Victory | level 2 |

If the waves are beaten before they set out (or `RESTORED_WAVE_ATTACK` is off), the sky goes straight to the storm at the
go-ahead. The severe rung (5) is not used: its visibility and radar penalty would last the whole assault.

## Inferences and open questions

- The 120 s before wave 1 sets out, and the very idea that the waves were meant to attack, are inferences from the dormant
  timer and the two authored paths ending at the base. A live look at whether `bdmisn07.aip` already sends those units
  (which would make the restored `Goto` redundant, not harmful) is open.
- Which of the duplicated `chin_scout1..3` the engine returns for `GetHandle` is not known; either trio works for the
  logic.
- Scaling is per (frame, entry path), a CR inference (the same rule as `misns2cr`); `liveCap = 16` is a guess.
- The fallback order (`attack_path2`, `attack_path1`) assumes both starts are usable hidden approaches; the first point
  of `attack_path1` is 470 m from the Black Dog base, so it is skipped while any human is in the base's west half.
- The idle-raider re-issue (20 s) costs one `GetCurrentCommand` per live raider; the strike beat per raid is a taste
  call, not a source fact.
- The out-of-lives path (`failDes`) is not exercised by the host tests.
