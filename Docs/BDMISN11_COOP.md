# Black Dog 11 co-op: Stranded (bd11cr)

Shared offline / 2-4 player co-op port of the Black Dog mission `bd11` on Elysium: escort the APC to the abandoned
Recycler and crew it, drive the Recycler to the geyser and hold it against six cloaked Chinese (CCA) waves, two
aerial drops and two raids, then run for the Chinese portal when it is set to blow. One `Missions/bd11cr.bzn`
and one `Scripts/bd11cr.lua` serve both modes. Companion to the faithful port underneath it
(`Scripts/bdmisn11.lua`, original C++ in `References/BlackDog11Source/`) and [`CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md`](CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md)
(the shared runtime). Pattern: [`MISNS2_COOP.md`](MISNS2_COOP.md) and [`BDMISN05_COOP.md`](BDMISN05_COOP.md). Status:
**host-tested only**; nothing here has run in the game (see "Live checklist").

## Teams and ownership

| Team | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 | Humans (host on 1). The authored Black Dog side (the start tank `player`, the `apc`, the escorts, the unmanned `recycler`, the armory) stays on team 1: shared, host-run. Extra starting recyclers are disabled. |
| 5 | The Chinese: the authored base, the six ambient raiders `enemy_1`..`enemy_6`, the turrets, the portal, and every wave unit. The body still says team 2; the runtime maps `BuildObject(..., 2, ...)` to team 5. |
| 7 | Support (nothing uses it here). |

Only the authority (host, or the offline player) runs the body; guests only present. `SetAIControl(5, true)` runs once
at chunk load. The mission has no `SetAIP`: the enemy is scripted (waves) plus the authored base, so there is no
shared enemy scrap pool; the human side's `SetScrap(1, 100)` becomes `100 + 10 per guest` (`scrapPerGuest`), and
`SetPilot(1, 10)` gives 10 pilots to every human team (each human respawns from its own pool).

### Humans as the player

The DLL's `user = GetPlayerHandle()` is assigned every frame and **never read** (the only use is
`SetPerceivedTeam(user, 2)` in the `TEST_EXPLOSION` block, a disabled debug build: `local TEST_EXPLOSION = false`). Every
order aims at the Recycler (the shared base) and every distance test is on the APC or the Recycler, so the nearest-human
token is not needed and `GetPlayerHandle` is left alone. For completeness `SetPerceivedTeam` maps a literal team 2 to 5 in
the runtime region (the dead block would otherwise make the user look like a team nobody belongs to).

## Scaling and concealed arrivals [owner rules, mission decisions]

Scaled spawns: all the Chinese craft the body builds: `cvfigh`, `cvltnk`, `cvtnk`, `cvhtnk`, `cvhraz`, `cvwalk` and the
`cssold` soldiers of the aerial drops. They become *tickets*: the body gets a negative request id, the unit is built by
the runtime on the next frames, and the body's later calls on it (`SetCloaked`, `Attack` the Recycler, `Defend2` an
attacker) are replayed on it and on its extras (a `Defend2` on an attacker that is itself still a ticket is rewritten to
the real handle when it is built). Not scaled: the APC pilot (`aspilo`) and the nav beacons (`bpcamr`, team 1) and the
authored map content.

Events. The runtime region wraps `BuildObject` once: **a new frame or a new entry path is a new scaling event**, and the
first unit of an event is mirrored by `min(floor(humans / 2), 2)` extras of the same kind, entry and orders. The events
are the five raiders of `drive_2` (when the Recycler passes `wave_trigger`), the four of `drive_1` (20 s after it sets
off), each of the six waves twice (the attackers at `attack_N`, the escort at `defend_N`), and the two aerial drops: 16
events, so 90 primaries plus `16 x extras`.

| Humans | Extras per event |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (offline) | 0 |
| 2 or 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 2 |

Cap: at most **24** live scaled units (`liveCap`; the runtime default is 12; the largest wave is 21 units plus their
extras). Extras are the only requests dropped for the cap, primaries always spawn.

No pop-in: a ticket is never built within 460 m of any human. Every authored staging point is 900 m or more from the
geyser the Recycler settles on **except** `drive_2` (about 510 m) and the aerial drop `aerial_1`, which sits 227 m from
that geyser. While the humans sit at the Recycler the drops are built at the first clear entry of `fallbackEntries`
(`attack_5`, `attack_1`, `drive_1`, `attack_4`, `attack_2`: far to the east and south-west of the base) instead, else they wait
(saved in `M.crcca`, so a save/load neither loses nor duplicates them). **Consequence, a design change:** the "aerial" soldiers
do not appear above the base but walk in from a hidden approach. All wave units are cloaked at spawn, as the body does (a
ticket is cloaked when the real unit is built).

The body builds the soldiers with `BuildObject("cssold", 2, "aerial_1", 400)`, a point index far past the one point of the
path (source quirk, kept in the faithful port). A ticket is built at the path (`Spawn` takes three arguments), so the index is
not passed on; a one-point path has only point 0.

## Humans' result, timer, win and loss

- Loss, unchanged and shared: the APC dies before the pilot is out (`bd11lsea`), the Recycler dies (`bd11lseb` once the
  countdown has run out, else `bd11lsed`), the transfer pilot dies (`bd11lsec`), the portal is destroyed before it blows
  (`bd11lsee`). The out-of-lives result fails for everyone (`failDes = "bd11lseb.des"`, the surrender debrief); a departed leader
  fails every guest.
- Win: unchanged. 26 minutes after the Recycler passes `wave_trigger` the evacuation order arrives (`bd11007`..`bd11012`, the
  objective turns to "get your units to the portal"), the countdown runs 90 s, the portal blows and `bd11011` ends the mission
  with `bd11win.des`. Nothing requires a human to be anywhere (the DLL's win is unconditional).
- `wonFlags = { "won", "explodePortal" }`: the blast has a 700 m damage radius (`xpltrso`) and the win follows it
  unconditionally, so a failure raised after the blast (a human killed by it, out of lives) is ignored; `endedFlags = { "won",
  "lost" }` stops further spawning.

## The countdown is host-counted [stock bug, co-op fix]

The map's mission class is `MultSTMission`, which restarts the cockpit timer upward every frame (see `BDMISN04_COOP.md`), so
`GetCockpitTimer() <= 0.0` can never be true: in the faithful port the portal would never blow, in co-op **or** offline on
this map. The CR layer shadows `StartCockpitTimer` / `GetCockpitTimer` / `HideCockpitTimer`: the host counts the 90 s
itself (saved with the mission state), and every peer shows the host's timer through the ordered stream.

## Cinematics are shared and unskippable in a network game [owner]

The opening (`camera_start` over the Recycler) is driven by `CameraPath` every frame, so the runtime's camera stream carries it
to every guest; the destruction shot (`camera_destruct` over the portal, one `CameraPath` call, speed 0) is re-sent by the
runtime every frame from the last frame. `CameraCancelled` is false in a network game; offline both stay skippable. The
opening ends when its path arrives.

**One deviation:** a skip key pressed by the *host* releases only the host's own camera and the runtime then stops driving
that shot (`CRCCARuntime.CameraPath` returns nothing once skipped). The opening ends on arrival, so the guests would stay in
the film for ever (the body never calls `CameraFinish`). The CR layer treats a host skip raised after the shot began as the
arrival (the opening ends for all). Guests cannot skip.

## Presentation survives a load [co-op fix]

The runtime's `Load` resets the presentation. Every published objective list, the portal marker, the nav names, the
countdown and a running film are remembered in `M.crcca.r` and published again after a load (`hooks.load` sets a flag, the
restored-content step runs it on the next frame): the objective list is replayed, `SetObjectiveName` on the Recycler nav
and the Portal beacon, `SetObjectiveOn(portal)`, the countdown with the time that is left, the opening camera if it was
running and the destruction shot if it was running. A load never replays a spawn, a line or a timer.

## Audio and local effects

- `IsAudioMessageDone` is wrapped so a nil or 0 handle counts as finished, and `AudioMessage` returns 0 for a failed
  start: the body's `~= nil` gates (`bd11008`/`bd11010`/`bd11012` chain, the win) cannot stall on a line that never played.
- `ColorFade` (the white flash) and the blast particles are local effects: every peer shows its own. The flash runs through
  the ordered stream on every peer; the five blasts (`xpltrsk` at `dw_1`..`dw_4`, `xpltrso` at the portal) are made by the
  host (with their damage) and **shown** by the guests as a presentation op (`BD11Boom`) that only guests apply.

## Stock bugs and co-op fixes in the CR layer (the faithful body is untouched)

- **The countdown never ends** (see above): `MultSTMission` makes `GetCockpitTimer()` count up.
- **Cloak on tickets.** The body's `SetCloaked(h)` right after the spawn would receive a negative request id: the shadow defers
  it and applies it when the real unit (and each of its extras) is built.
- **Host skip strands the guests in the opening** (see "Cinematics").
- **Audio handles** (see "Audio"): a failed line cannot stall the radio chain or the win.
- **Light and sound of the portal are local** (see "Audio and local effects").
- **The blast cannot fail the mission** (`wonFlags`).
- **Pilots and scrap** per human team / per guest (see "Teams").
- **Objectives, markers and the countdown after a load** (see "Presentation survives a load").
- The body already carries its own documented port fixes (null-handle guards, the missing second aerial drop, the pilot
  health reset, the path end at the TRO 1.3 12 m 2D radius).

Stock fix, post-blast Recycler loss: a Recycler destroyed after the blast sets the body's `lost` flag, the shared
failure is ignored by `wonFlags`, and the body's win check needs `not M.lost`, so stock would hang. `Restored.Step`
finishes the win itself once `bd11011.wav` is done (`explodePortal`, `lost`, not `won`).

## Weather: Elysium

Elysium, the Earth-like world under a blue-grey overcast: the existing `CRElysiumWeather` director (the Venus engine
re-dressed: blue-grey fog and light, cool white-blue rain, no sulphur, vents or acid on the glass, gentler wind and strikes)
with the generated `CRElysiumSitesBD11` sites, networked exactly as Venus in `misns2cr` (seeded timeline from the host, host-chosen
strikes over the ordered stream with the ops `VenusState`, `VenusStrike`, `VenusErupt`, `VenusFog`, `Save`/`Load` state,
`AnnounceState`). Lighting is the runtime default (subtle). If the weather module or the sites module is missing, or any weather
call errors, the mission runs without weather and logs once. The ladder starts at level 1 (calm) and stays there until the
story moves. Beats (host/offline only, each once, saved in `M.crcca`; the director never damages, a strike only knocks craft
near it about):

| Moment | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| The pilot boards the Recycler (`bd11002`) | level 2 (haze) |
| The Recycler passes `wave_trigger`, the first raiders drive in | level 3 (rising) |
| The third wave is sent (9 min) | level 4 (storm) |
| First aerial drop (8 min), second (13 min) | a strike over the Recycler each |
| The last wave (21 min) | a strike over the Recycler |
| The portal countdown starts | level 5 (severe) for the 90 s run |
| The portal blows | a strike over the portal |
| Victory | level 2 |

## Inferences and open questions

- Scaling is per (frame, entry path), a CR inference (the same rule as `misns2cr`); `liveCap = 24` is a guess for four humans on
  the late, overlapping waves.
- The fallback order assumes the east and south-west approaches are usable and that the humans stay near the geyser; a watched
  fallback is skipped for the next.
- The soldiers of the aerial drops arrive on foot from a hidden approach when the humans sit at the base (a design change);
  whether point 400 on `aerial_1` ever worked in the engine is unverified.
- Guests showing the blast as a local `MakeExplosion` assumes the particles of a script-made explosion are not networked (if
  they are, a guest sees them twice); the damage is applied only by the host's call.
- The HUD countdown on peers: if the engine keeps restarting it upward (the `MultSTMission` override) the number on screen is
  wrong while the logic is right (same caveat as `bd04cr`).
- Who may command the shared APC and Recycler (team 1 objects) from a guest's team is the runtime's shared-base behaviour, as
  in the other Black Dog ports; the escort objective needs it.
- The 0.75 trigger of `bd11003` and the warning colour (red) of `bd11004.otf` are inferences.
