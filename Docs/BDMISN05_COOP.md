# Black Dog 05 co-op: Achilles Heel (bd05cr)

Shared offline / 2-4 player co-op port of the Black Dog mission `bd05`: hold the Black Dog base on Ganymede while
the Chinese (CCA) send four cloaked waves, two rear attacks and an artillery pair against it, destroy everything on
the Chinese base, then watch the survivors flee through their portal. One `Missions/bd05cr.bzn` and one
`Scripts/bd05cr.lua` serve both modes. Companion to [`BD05_LUA_PORT.md`](BD05_LUA_PORT.md) (the faithful DLL port
underneath, `Scripts/bd05.lua`) and [`CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md`](CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md) (the shared runtime). Pattern:
[`MISNS2_COOP.md`](MISNS2_COOP.md). Status: **host-tested only**; nothing here has run in the game (see "Live
checklist").

## Teams and ownership

| Team | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 | Humans (host on 1). The authored Black Dog side (the start tank `player`, the recycler, the two base turrets `bd_turret1/2`) stays on team 1: shared, host-run. Extra starting recyclers are disabled. |
| 5 | The Chinese: the authored base, the 15 ambient units `unit_1`..`unit_15`, the 48 proximity mines, the portal, and every wave unit and quitter. The body still says team 2; the runtime maps `BuildObject(..., 2, ...)` to team 5 (the retreat column, built by hand, does the same). |
| 7 | Support (nothing uses it here). |

Only the authority (host, or the offline player) runs the body; guests only present. `SetAIControl(5, true)` runs once
at chunk load. The mission has no `SetAIP`: the enemy is scripted (waves) plus the authored base, so there is no
shared enemy scrap pool; the human side's `SetScrap(1, 8)` becomes `8 + 10 per guest` (`scrapPerGuest`), and
`SetPilot(1, 10)` gives 10 pilots to every human team (each human respawns from its own pool).

### Humans as the player

The DLL's `user = GetPlayerHandle()` is assigned every frame and **never read**: no gate, order or camera aims at
"the player". Every wave aims at the recycler (the shared base), so the nearest-human token is not needed and
`GetPlayerHandle` is left alone. (When the recycler is gone, the stock-bug fix below aims the waves at the nearest
human.)

## Scaling and concealed arrivals [owner rules, mission decisions]

Scaled spawns: all six Chinese combat ODFs: `cvfigh`, `cvltnk`, `cvtnk`, `cvhraz`, `cvwalk`, `cvartl`. They become
*tickets*: the body gets a negative request id, the unit is built by the runtime on the next frames, and the body's
later calls on it (`SetCloaked`, `Goto`/`Attack` against the recycler at priority 1) are replayed on it and on its
extras. Not scaled: the six retreat-column units (path `quitters`) and the authored map content.

Events. The runtime region wraps `BuildObject` once: **a new frame or a new entry path is a new scaling event**, and
the first unit of an event is mirrored by `min(floor(humans / 2), 2)` extras of the same kind, entry and orders. The
events are the artillery pair (420 s), rear attack 1 (450 s), wave 2 (540 s), rear attack 2 (660 s), wave 3 (840 s),
wave 4 (1140 s) and wave 1 (300 s): seven events, so 31 primaries plus `7 x extras`.

| Humans | Extras per event |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (offline) | 0 |
| 2 or 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 2 |

Cap: at most **20** live scaled units (`liveCap`; the runtime default is 12). Extras are the only requests dropped for
the cap, primaries always spawn.

No pop-in: a ticket is never built within 460 m of any human. The two rear attacks (`rear_attack`, 304 m from the
recycler) and the artillery (`howit`, 113 m) are *authored next to the Black Dog base*, so while the humans sit in the
base they would arrive in view. They use the first clear entry of `fallbackEntries` (`first_wave`, `fourth_wave`,
`third_wave`, `second_wave`, 728 m or more from the start) instead, else wait (saved in `M.crcca`, so a save/load
neither loses nor duplicates them). **Consequence, a design change:** while every human is within 460 m of those two
points, the "rear" attack and the artillery come from the front, like the other waves. A human who leaves the base
makes the authored points clear again and they come from behind as designed. All wave units are cloaked at spawn, as
the body does (a ticket is cloaked when the real unit is built).

Everything else the body builds is scenery of the film: the retreat column (see below).

## Humans' result, timer, win and loss

- Win: unchanged. All 46 tracked enemies dead after wave 4 (`bd05004`) -> six retreating units, portal pulse
  (`bd05005`, retreat film) -> `bd05006` -> `SucceedMission(..., "bd05win.des")` once (the runtime holds the result
  until every guest acknowledged the preceding events).
- "All dead" also waits for the co-op **extras** and for requests still waiting for a hidden entry (the body only knows
  its own 46 units): see "Stock bugs".
- Loss: the mission has none. The only failure is the co-op one: out of lives (every human out) fails for everyone, and
  a departed leader fails every guest. The game ships no `bd05` loss debrief, so the out-of-lives result has no
  description (`failDes` unset).
- `wonFlags`/`endedFlags = { "won" }`: once the final narration starts a late failure is ignored and nothing more is built.

## The portal

The inward pulse (`PortalIn(portal)`) is a local effect of the object, so the CR layer starts it on every peer
through the ordered presentation stream (`presentationOps.PortalIn`, each peer pulses its own copy of the map
object `portal`). Nobody travels through this portal: the retreating column is removed by `IsTouching` on the host
(the removal replicates), so there is no per-human teleport to handle.

## Cinematics are shared and unskippable in a network game [owner]

Both films (the opening arc over the base, the retreat film on the portal) are driven by `CameraPath` every frame, so
the runtime's camera stream carries them to every guest; `CameraCancelled` is false in a network game, offline both
stay skippable. The opening ends when its path arrives; the retreat film ends 15 s after it starts and 3 s after
`bd05005` (host-side timers).

**One deviation:** a skip key pressed by the *host* releases only the host's own camera and the runtime then stops
driving that shot (`CRCCARuntime.CameraPath` returns nothing once skipped). The opening ends on arrival, so the film,
the retreat movie behind it (`cameraComplete[0]`) and the victory would stall for everyone. The CR layer treats a host
skip during the opening as the arrival (the opening ends for all). Guests cannot skip. A save/load cannot resume a
film (the camera is gone): on load a film that was running opens its camera again and the body ends it as usual.

The six retreating units at path `quitters` are the stage of the retreat film: built directly on team 5 (no extras,
no waiting for concealment), in the frame the film starts.

## Stock bugs and co-op fixes in the CR layer (the faithful body is untouched)

- **Everything beaten.** The body decides "all dead" from its own 46 handles; the co-op extras that mirror each wave
  and requests still waiting for a hidden entry are not among them, so the base could be "cleared" with enemies alive
  (or with a wave not yet built). A one-line region before `M.objective2Complete = allDead` makes the test also wait
  for the runtime's live extras and waiting arrivals (`Restored.EnemiesLeft`).
- **No recycler, idle waves.** `SpawnUnit` orders a unit against `M.recycler` only while it stands, and a unit whose
  target dies stops. With the recycler gone (or a new one built by a human) every wave unit sat idle where it was
  built. While `M.recycler` is gone, every living wave unit (and extra) is sent `Attack(nearest human, 1)`, re-issued
  every 20 s (`Restored.Retarget`); no effect while the recycler stands.
- **Cloak on tickets.** The body's `SetCloaked(h)` right after the spawn would receive a negative request id: the
  shadow defers it and applies it when the real unit (and each of its extras) is built.
- **Host skip stalls the opening** (see "Cinematics").
- **Portal pulse is local** (see "The portal").
- **Pilots and scrap** per human team / per guest (see "Teams").
- **Camera after a load** reopens (see "Cinematics").
- The body already carries its own documented port fixes (`BD05_LUA_PORT.md`: the orphaned objective 1 completes
  with the all-dead event, absolute `SucceedMission` time, null-handle guards, missing narration).

## Weather: Ganymede

Ganymede, the icy airless Jovian moon: the existing `CREuropaWeather` director (no new module) with the generated
`CRGanymedeSitesBD05` sites (three cryo-volcanic vents next to the Black Dog base, frost anchors, bounds), networked
exactly as in `bd02cr`/`misn12cr` (seeded timeline from the host, host-chosen events over the ordered stream with
the ops `EuropaState`, `EuropaPlume`, `EuropaQuake`, `EuropaSurge`, `Save`/`Load` state, `AnnounceState`). Lighting is
the orbit mode (`period = 1800`, `startHour = -40`) and the sensor modifier is bound once the director exists
(`Environment.BindWeatherSensors("Ganymede", ...)`, again after a load). If the weather module or the sites module is
missing, or any weather call errors, the mission runs without weather and logs once. With zero vents the plumes are
simply skipped. Beats (host/offline only, each once; the director never damages):

| Moment | Beat |

| Wave 2 attacks (540 s) | level 3, quake 0.35 / 3 s, plume at `eggeizr14_geyser` |

## Inferences and open questions

- Scaling is per (frame, entry path), a CR inference (the same rule as `misns2cr`); `liveCap = 20` is a guess for four
  humans on the late, overlapping waves.
- The fallback order (`first_wave`, `fourth_wave`, `third_wave`, `second_wave`) assumes all four are usable approaches
  and that the humans stay in the east half of the base zone; a watched fallback is skipped for the next.
- The "contact" radius of `bd05003` (600 m) and its 90 s fallback are inferences; nothing in the source places the line.
- Treating the host's skip of the opening as its arrival assumes a skip press is visible to `CameraCancelled()` in the
  same frame the runtime releases the camera.
- The out-of-lives path is not exercised by the host tests (the runtime flag `coopFailed` is set directly).
- The retarget re-issue (20 s) and the 600 m contact test cost one `GetDistance` per unit; they run only when needed.
