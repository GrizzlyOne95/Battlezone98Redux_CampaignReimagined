# Black Dog 10 co-op: The Enemy Within (bd10cr)

Shared offline / 2-4 player co-op port of the Black Dog mission `bd10`: on Elysium the Black Dogs must destroy six key
Chinese (CCA) buildings while eight hidden groups ambush anything that comes within 400 m; when the last building falls
a nav beacon calls them back to the drop zone, where the Chinese capture the recycler and the mission ends. One
`Missions/bd10cr.bzn` and one `Scripts/bd10cr.lua` serve both modes. Companion to
[`References/BlackDog10Source/README.md`](../References/BlackDog10Source/README.md) (the faithful DLL port underneath,
`Scripts/bdmisn10.lua`) and [`CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md`](CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md) (the shared runtime). Pattern:
[`MISNS2_COOP.md`](MISNS2_COOP.md), [`BDMISN05_COOP.md`](BDMISN05_COOP.md). Status: **host-tested only**; nothing here
has run in the game (see "Live checklist").

## Teams and ownership

| Team | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 | Humans (host on 1). The authored Black Dog side (the start tank `player`, four tanks, four fighters, the armory `bd_armory`) stays on team 1: shared, host-run. Extra starting recyclers are disabled. |
| 5 | The Chinese: the authored base (the six key buildings `destroy_1`..`destroy_6`, towers, power plants, turret tanks, walkers) and every ambusher. The body still says team 2; the runtime maps `BuildObject(..., 2, ...)` and `SetScrap(2, 0)` to team 5. |
| 0 | The neutral portal the body builds with the APC. |
| 7 | Support (nothing uses it here). |

Only the authority (host, or the offline player) runs the body; guests only present. `SetAIControl(5, true)` runs once
at chunk load. The mission has no `SetAIP`; the human side's `SetScrap(1, 100)` becomes `100 + 10 per guest`
(`scrapPerGuest`), and `SetPilot(1, 10)` gives 10 pilots to every human team (each human respawns from its own pool).
Respawn point: the path `backup`, 100 m from the start (no recycler exists until the finale).

### Humans as the player

The DLL's `user = GetPlayerHandle()` is assigned every frame and **never read**: nothing aims at "the player". The
only place the body asks about the Black Dogs is the ambush trigger (see "Stock bugs").

## Scaling and concealed arrivals [owner rules, mission decisions]

Scaled spawns: the five Chinese combat ODFs the ambushes use (`cvfigh`, `cvltnk`, `cvtnk`, `cvwalk`, `cvhtnk`). They
become *tickets*: the body gets a negative request id, the unit is built by the runtime on the next frames, and the
body's `Hunt` is replayed on it and on its extras. Not scaled: the nav beacon, the APC, the portal, the recycler and
its six `cvtnka` escorts (scenery of the finale, built directly with the body's own teams 0 and 1).

Events. A new frame or a new spawn path is a new scaling event: the first unit of each ambush is mirrored by
`min(floor(humans / 2), 2)` extras of the same kind, entry and orders. Eight ambushes, 46 primaries plus
`8 x extras` (0 offline, 1 for 2-3 humans, 2 for 4). Cap: at most **20** live scaled units (`liveCap`); extras are the
only requests dropped for the cap, primaries always spawn.

No pop-in: the runtime default (460 m) is wider than the 400 m ambush trigger, which would hold every ambush until the
humans left, so this mission sets `safeRadius = 350`. A ticket is built only when every human is at least 350 m from
the spot. **Consequence, a design change:** an ambush whose trigger was tripped by a Black Dog unit (the AI wingmen
count) while a human stands within 350 m of the spot waits (saved in `M.crcca`, so a save/load neither loses nor
duplicates it) until the spot is clear. No fallback entries: an ambush has exactly one authored spot.

## Humans' result, timer, win and loss

- Win: unchanged. All six targets down -> 10 s -> nav beacon and `bd10002` -> 5 s -> APC and portal, 7 s APC film ->
  recycler and escort, `bd10003` + 10 s closing film -> `SucceedMission(+1 s, "bd10win.des")` once (the runtime holds
  the result until every guest acknowledged the preceding events).
- Loss: the mission has none. The only failure is the co-op one: out of lives (every human out) fails for everyone, and
  a departed leader fails every guest. The game ships no `bd10` loss debrief, so the out-of-lives result has no
  description (`failDes` unset).
- `wonFlags`/`endedFlags = { "crFinale" }`: the CR layer latches `M.crFinale` when the closing film is complete, so a
  late failure is ignored and nothing more is built. (The DLL's own `won` flag is never set; it is left alone.)

## The portal

Nobody travels through the portal: the DLL builds a neutral `cbport` next to the APC and sends the APC and recycler
along their paths; there is no teleport and no per-human transport to carry across. It is scenery of the closing film.

## Cinematics are shared and unskippable in a network game [owner]

All three films (the opening arc over the first target, the 7 s APC shot, the closing shot on the captured recycler)
are driven by `CameraPath` every frame, so the runtime's camera stream carries them to every guest; `CameraCancelled`
is false in a network game, offline each stays skippable. A save/load cannot resume a film (the camera is gone): on
load a film that was running opens its camera again and the body ends it as usual.

**One deviation:** a skip key pressed by the *host* releases only the host's own camera and the runtime then stops
driving that shot (`CRCCARuntime.CameraPath` returns nothing once skipped). The opening ends on arrival (and then on the
line plus two seconds), so it would stall for everyone. The CR layer treats a host skip in a network game as the
arrival; only a cancel raised after the shot began counts (the flag is sampled when `CameraReady` opens the shot).
Guests cannot skip.

## Restored content [owner decision 2026-11-01]

Cut-content restorations live only in the CR layer, apply offline and in co-op, and sit behind
`RESTORED_FACTORY_OBJECTIVES` (top of the runtime region of `bd10cr.lua`). Each is gated on what the shipped game
carries.

**Unused voice lines: none.** The game ships `bd10001.wav`..`bd10003.wav` (all played by the DLL) and no other `bd10`
audio; `Text/` carries the three subtitles. Nothing to slot in.

**Unused objective texts: restored.** `StockODFFiles` ships `bd10002.otf`..`bd10007.otf` ("Factory 1".."Factory 6"),
which the DLL never uses; the briefing `bd10001` ("six key buildings that must be destroyed ... Their locations will be
forwarded to you") promises exactly that list and the markers. With the flag on:

| Block | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Objective list | `bd10001.otf` plus `bd10002..07.otf`, one per target (`destroy_N` is "Factory N"); an entry turns green when its target is destroyed (the body's own test: missing, deleted or zero health), `bd10001` turns green with the last. |
| Markers | `SetObjectiveOn` on each living target at the start (and again after a load); replicated to every peer by the runtime. |

The faithful body's single objective (`ClearObjectives(); AddObjective("bd10001.otf", "white")`) stays; the CR step
republishes the list when it changes (the runtime drops identical rebuilds).

Source blocks considered and **not** restored:

| Block | Source | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `//CameraFinish();` in the closing shot | `Execute`, camera 3: the shot is left active across the one-second victory transition | Kept as is (the port documents it as deliberate). |
| `// don't do this part after the first shot` / `navDelay = 999999.9f` | `Execute`, start block | Comments around the `startDone` latch; no behaviour. |
| `lost`, `won` members | `BlackDog10Mission` | Never read or written by the DLL; no new gate. |

Authored map content the script never uses (documented, not wired): the paths `edge_path` and `outer_limits` (map
bounds), the 16 anonymous (unlabelled) paths, the Black Dog armory `bd_armory` (map-native base content), the towers,
power plants and turret tanks (map-native enemy content), the seven geysers (the weather sites use them).

## Stock bugs and co-op fixes in the CR layer (the faithful body is untouched)

- **Ambush trigger watches team 1 only.** `GetNearestUnitOnTeam(spot, 0, 1)` ignores humans on teams 2-4, so a guest
  could walk into every ambush unseen (and the trap fired only for the host). The shadow asks all four human teams and
  returns the nearest.
- **Pilots.** `SetPilot(1, 10)` gives the pilots to every human team.
- **Absent narration.** A failed `AudioMessage` returned `nil`; the nav gate (`navSound ~= nil and done`) would never
  open and the finale never start. The shadow returns `0` and `IsAudioMessageDone(nil or 0)` counts as finished;
  `StopAudioMessage(0)` is ignored.
- **Host skip stalls the opening** (see "Cinematics").
- **Objectives and markers after a load.** The runtime's `Load` resets the presentation; `Restored.Step` republishes
  the objective list and the target markers (and reopens a running camera).
- **Scrap.** `SetScrap(2, 0)` is the Chinese pool: team 5, never a guest's team 2 (the runtime maps it).
- The body already carries its own documented port fix (missing/deleted targets count as destroyed).

## Weather: Elysium

Elysium, the Earth-like storm world of the Black Dog campaign: `CRElysiumWeather` (the Venus engine in its blue-grey
dress; no sulphur, no volcano) over the generated `CRElysiumSitesBD10` sites (seven geysers, bounds), networked with
the shared Venus ops over the ordered stream (host-chosen events, `Save`/`Load` state, `AnnounceState`). The module's
default ops are used. If the weather module or the sites module is missing, or any weather call errors, the mission
runs without weather and logs once. Beats (host/offline only):

| Moment | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| Start | level 2 (haze, building from calm) |
| An ambush springs (each of 8) | a strike on its spot; the first: level 3 |
| Four ambushes sprung, or three buildings down | level 4 (storm) |
| A key building destroyed (each of 6) | a strike on its ruins |
| The APC is called to the drop zone | level 5 (severe) and a strike over the landing |
| The Chinese take the recycler | a strike over the landing |
| The closing film is complete | level 4: the storm eases for the debrief |

## Inferences and open questions

- `safeRadius = 350` and `liveCap = 20` are inferences (a margin under the 400 m trigger; four humans on overlapping
  ambushes).
- "Factory N" is matched to `destroy_N` by index; the shipped texts do not say which building is which.
- Treating the host's skip of the opening as its arrival assumes a skip press is visible to `CameraCancelled()` in the
  same frame the runtime releases the camera; the "stale cancel" guard samples that flag when the shot opens.
- The out-of-lives path is not exercised by the host tests (the runtime flag `coopFailed` is set directly).
- The weather level choices (which ambush count is "the storm") are art-direction guesses.

## Live checklist (unrun)

   briefing objective and six "Factory" entries appear, six markers show on every peer.
2. Walk a guest (team 2-4) alone toward a defence group: the ambush springs at about 400 m and the units appear out of
   sight (350 m+), hunting; 4-player: extras scale, none visible at spawn.
3. Elysium beats: a strike on each sprung ambush and each destroyed building, rising storm, no weather error in the log.
4. Destroy each building: its entry turns green on every peer; after the sixth, 10 s, nav beacon and `bd10002`, 5 s,
   APC and portal, shared APC film.
5. Recycler and six tanks at the drop zone, `bd10003`, shared closing film, one shared victory with `bd10win.des`.
6. Save during the intro, during an ambush wait and after three buildings: the objective list and markers return after
   the load, a running film reopens, no ambush repeats.
7. Out of lives: one shared failure (no description).
