# Black Dog 15 co-op: The Best Laid Plans (bd15cr)

Shared offline / 2-4 player co-op port of the Black Dog mission `bd15`: on Ganymede the Black Dogs must keep the
Chinese (CCA) from reaching their launch-site transport while six waves close in from every side; when the last
attacker is down the Black Dogs are told to get clear, and a 30 second countdown ends in an orbital strike on the
transport. One `Missions/bd15cr.bzn` and one `Scripts/bd15cr.lua` serve both modes. Companion to
[`References/BlackDog15Source/PORT_NOTES.md`](../References/BlackDog15Source/PORT_NOTES.md) (the faithful DLL port
underneath, `Scripts/bdmisn15.lua`) and [`CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md`](CCA_COOP_RUNTIME.md) (the shared runtime). Pattern:
[`MISNS2_COOP.md`](MISNS2_COOP.md), [`BDMISN05_COOP.md`](BDMISN05_COOP.md), [`BDMISN10_COOP.md`](BDMISN10_COOP.md).
Status: **host-tested only**; nothing here has run in the game (see "Live checklist").

## Teams and ownership

| Team | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 | Humans (host on 1). The authored Black Dog side (the start tank `bvtank11_wingman`, ten wingmen, the armory `bvslfd0_armory`) stays on team 1: shared, host-run. Extra starting recyclers are disabled. |
| 5 | The Chinese: the launch pad `launch` (the transport), its power plants and turrets, and every attacker. The body still says team 2; the runtime maps `BuildObject(..., 2, ...)` to team 5. |
| 0 | The neutral, hidden camera target the finale builds (`apcamr`). |
| 7 | Support (nothing uses it here). |

Only the authority (host, or the offline player) runs the body; guests only present. `SetAIControl(5, true)` runs once
at chunk load. The mission has no `SetAIP` and never sets the enemy's scrap; the human side's `SetScrap(1, 50)` becomes
`50 + 10 per guest` (`scrapPerGuest`), and `SetPilot(1, 10)` gives 10 pilots to every human team (each human respawns
from its own pool). Respawn point: the armory `bvslfd0_armory`, the shared Black Dog base in the start group.

### Humans as the player

The DLL's `user = GetPlayerHandle()` is assigned every frame and read in two places: the "keep away from that thing"
warning (nearer than 300 m to the launch site) and, in the cut code, the safe-distance check. In co-op the body's
`user` is the runtime's nearest-human token (`GetPlayerHandle`, `GetDistance` and `IsValid` are shadowed): the warning
plays for every peer when **any** human comes within 300 m of the launch site, and the safe-distance check measures the
**nearest** human, so "safe" means every human is beyond 800 m. Nothing in the body gives an order to "the player".

## Scaling and concealed arrivals [owner rules, mission decisions]

Scaled spawns: the seven Chinese ODFs the six waves use (`cvfigh`, `cvltnk`, `cvtnk`, `cvapc`, `cvhtnk`, `cvhraz`,
`cvhaul`). They become *tickets*: the body gets a negative request id, the unit is built by the runtime on the next
frames, and the body's `Goto` and `SetObjectiveOn` are replayed on it and on its extras. Not scaled: the hidden
`apcamr` finale camera target (built directly, neutral).

Events. A new frame or a new spawn path is a new scaling event: the first unit of each wave is mirrored by
`min(floor(humans / 2), 2)` extras of the same kind, spawn and orders. There are eight events (west scout, south
column, north squad, east tanks, east follow-up, and the last run's south, north and west groups): 32 primaries plus
`8 x extras` (0 offline, 1 for 2-3 humans, 2 for 4). Cap: at most **30** live scaled units (`liveCap`); extras are the
only requests dropped for the cap, primaries always spawn.

No pop-in: a ticket is built only when every human is at least 460 m from its spawn (the runtime default
`safeRadius`). The four spawns are 1000-2400 m from the start, so this only matters once the humans move out; a wave
whose spawn has a human within 460 m comes from the next hidden spawn (`fallbackEntries`: east, north, west, south)
and still marches its own road. Every attacker, extras included, gets the body's objective marker when it is built.

## Humans' result, timer, win and loss

- Win: unchanged. All 32 attackers (and their extras) dead -> `bd15007`, objective list green + escape objective ->
  30 s countdown -> white flash and the orbital strike -> `SucceedMission(+5 s, "bd15win.des")` once (the runtime
  holds the result until every guest acknowledged the preceding events).
- Loss: a living attacker within 100 m of `chin_launch` latches the breach (`bd15011`, +5 s, `bd15012`, then
  `FailMission(now, "bd15lose.des")`), exactly as in the DLL port; a breach excludes the win. The co-op out-of-lives
  failure also uses `bd15lose.des` (`failDes`).
- `wonFlags = { "doingExplosion" }`: once the blast has gone off a late failure is ignored.
- `endedFlags = { "doingExplosion", "wonLost" }`: no more scaled units are built once the blast has gone off or the
  breach has latched, so a wave timer that runs out during `bd15011`/`bd15012` spawns nothing (stock fix: the body's
  wave timers ignore the breach).

## The portal

There is no portal in this mission: nobody is carried anywhere. The only scripted placement is the neutral camera
target at the blast point.

## Cinematics are shared and unskippable in a network game [owner]

The mission has one film, the finale shot at 2 s on the countdown (`CameraPath("camera_finale", 2400, 0, target)`,
re-issued every frame by the body). The runtime's camera stream carries it to every guest; `CameraCancelled` is false
in a network game, offline the shot stays skippable. The body never waits for the shot to arrive (the camera is
held until the mission transition), so a host skip, which releases only the host's own camera, changes nothing for
the others and needs no guard. A save/load cannot resume the shot (the camera is gone): the CR layer opens it again
on load and the body keeps issuing it.

## Restored content [owner decision 2026-11-01]

of the runtime region of `bd15cr.lua`: `RESTORED_COUNTDOWN_LINES` and `RESTORED_SAFE_DISTANCE`. The original C++
with its commented-out code is in `References/BlackDog15Source/BlackDog15Mission.cpp`.

ten of them; `bd15008.wav`, `bd15009.wav` and `bd15013.wav` appear nowhere except one commented `bd15013.wav` call

## Stock bugs and co-op fixes in the CR layer (the faithful body is untouched)

- **The countdown never ends on this map's clock.** The co-op map runs `MultSTMission`, which restarts the cockpit
  timer upward every frame, so `GetCockpitTimer() <= 0` is never true: no finale camera, no explosion, no victory,
  offline and in co-op (the faithful-port run in `Test-Bdmisn15Coop.lua` demonstrates it). The CR layer shadows
  `StartCockpitTimer` / `GetCockpitTimer` / `HideCockpitTimer`: the host counts the 30 s itself (saved with the
  mission) and shows the same timer on every peer. After the countdown the timer reads zero.
- **Proximity and distance trigger watch the host only.** The warning `bd15010` and the safe-distance check use
  `GetDistance(user, ...)`; `user` is now the nearest human.
- **Mirrors are invisible to the body.** The extras that mirror each wave are not in the body's unit table: the
  "everything is dead" gate also waits for them (and for requests still held for a hidden spawn), and a mirror within
  100 m of the launch site breaches it like any attacker.
- **Absent narration stalls the waves.** The body keeps a line only when its handle is neither `nil` nor `0`; a failed
  `AudioMessage` would never open the next wave's delay. The shadow returns a placeholder handle the body accepts, and
  `IsAudioMessageDone(nil / 0 / placeholder)` counts as finished.
- **Flash and blast are local effects.** `ColorFade` and `MakeExplosion` are presented on every peer (the host makes the
  damaging blast, a guest its own copy of the particles).
- **Pilots.** `SetPilot(1, 10)` gives the pilots to every human team.
- **Markers on tickets.** A marker asked for a unit that is still a ticket waits for the real unit (extras too).
- **Objectives, markers, countdown and camera after a load.** The runtime's `Load` resets the presentation;
  `Restored.AfterLoad` republishes the objective list from the saved state, marks every living attacker again,
  re-issues the running timer with its remaining time and opens the finale camera again.
- **Wave audio:** the body already carries its own documented port fixes (dead handles never breach, a breach excludes
  the win, the finale shot is re-issued every frame).
- Not fixed (map): `chin_launch` is authored twice, a one-point path at the pad and a nine-point loop to its west. The
  engine takes the first (the file orders the one-point path first), which is what the 100 m and 300 m rings assume;
  `Test-Bdmisn15CoopContract.py` pins that order.

## Weather: Ganymede

Ganymede, the icy airless Jovian moon: the existing `CREuropaWeather` director (no new module) with the generated
`CRGanymedeSitesBD15` sites (six cryo-volcanic vents, frost anchors, bounds), networked as in `bd05cr`: seeded
timeline from the host, host-chosen events over the ordered stream with the ops `EuropaState`, `EuropaPlume`,
`EuropaQuake`, `EuropaSurge`, `Save`/`Load` state, `AnnounceState`. Lighting is the orbit mode (`period = 1800`,
`startHour = -40`) and the sensor modifier is bound once the director exists
(`Environment.BindWeatherSensors("Ganymede", ...)`, again after a load). If the weather module or the sites module is
missing, or any weather call errors, the mission runs without weather and logs once. Beats (host/offline only, each
once; the director never damages), tied to the attack waves and the story:

## Inferences and open questions

- `liveCap = 30` is an inference (the east waves, the north squad and the last run overlap at four humans).
- The safe-distance restoration treats "the nearest human beyond 800 m" as the co-op form of the source's single
  `user` check; the escape objective turning green is an inference from the unused `objective2Complete` flag.
- The 2 s victory hold is a deviation forced by the restored `bd15009`; it is gone with the flag.
- The hidden `apcamr` camera target is built by the host and shown to guests by label; whether it stays out of the
  guests' radar and AI targeting is not verified (as in the faithful port's note).
- The weather level choices are art-direction guesses; the director has no gameplay effect.
- The out-of-lives path is not exercised by the host tests (the runtime flag `coopFailed` is set by the runtime).
